# Mechanical Design

This folder contains the mechanical design documentation for the Line Following Robot.

## Chassis

- Material: PVC
- Thickness: 4 mm
- Width: 100 mm
- Length: 147 mm
- Wheel cut-outs: 20 × 47 mm
- Sensor opening: 90 × 5 mm
- Front bumper: 5 mm
- Rear bumper: 5 mm

The chassis layout shows the planned position of the motors, wheels, LiPo battery, STM32, buck converters, HM-10 Bluetooth module and six line sensors.
